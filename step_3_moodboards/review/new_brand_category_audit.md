# New-brand category coverage audit

## Implemented now

| Brand | Existing category | Added category | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Balance | clothes | shoes | Footwear is the primary, most recognizable product family. |
| Nike | clothes | shoes | Footwear is a core category with materially different visual language from apparel. |
| Puma | clothes | shoes | Footwear is a core category spanning motorsport, terrace, lifestyle and performance. |
| Burton | clothes | shoes | Snowboard boots are a core technical category and require their own product-led board. |

## Keep as currently scoped

The dedicated shoe labels (ABRA, Air Jordan, Asics, Golden Goose, Birkenstock, EYTYS, Geox, Gia Borghini, Church's, Crocs, Clarks, Ecco, Converse, Carvela, Diemme and Hunter) remain shoe-only. The dedicated jewellery labels remain jewellery-only. Apparel-first labels remain clothes-only when their other products are capsule or supporting lines rather than a defining category.

## Strong candidates for a later dedicated expansion review

These labels have meaningful additional categories, but they should not be multiplied automatically without their own reference review and moodboard pass: Bimba Y Lola (bags, jewellery and shoes), Burberry (bags and shoes), Diesel (bags and shoes), Gucci (bags and jewellery; shoes already present), Karl Lagerfeld (bags and shoes), Lacoste (shoes), Loro Piana (bags and shoes), McQueen (bags, shoes and jewellery), Michael Kors (bags; shoes already exist in the base data), Moncler (shoes), Moschino (bags and shoes), and A Bathing Ape (shoes).

Policy: create a second category only when the brand has a substantial current assortment and a category-specific visual identity. Selling a few accessories is not enough.
